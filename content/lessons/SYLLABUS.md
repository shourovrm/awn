# Learn syllabus: reading and tajweed

The Learn tab has one guide of 19 units in two parts. Part 1 (units 1 to 10) teaches reading in the order of the Noorani Qaida (*Al-Qaida An-Nooraniyya*, Qari Noor Muhammad). Part 2 (units 11 to 19) teaches tajweed, the rules of reciting, in the order of *Tajweed Made Easy* (Usama Mahida, compiled by the teachers of SMA, 2018, 31 pages), with facts that are missing from the book taken from Wikibooks *Arabic*. Every unit text is our own wording; both books are credited in `content/lessons/README.md`, the repository README and the app's Sources page.

## Unit layout

Each unit is a teaching page and a practice set. The teaching page is a grid of the unit's letters or syllables, as on the Qaida page: a tap on a cell plays its sound, and the transliteration under each cell is in the app language. Play all reads the grid in order and lights each cell. The practice set draws 10 items at random from the unit's pool, so a repeat visit gives different items; an item is either a row to read aloud and then hear, or a sound to hear and pick from four cells. Transliteration is hidden in practice, because reading without it is the skill being tested.

## Sources of sound

Units 1 to 10 use the recordings of the Islamic Home Schooling Academy of New Zealand (Noorani Qaida lessons 1 to 17, CC BY), cut into one clip per Qaida cell. Units 11 to 19 use the app's word-by-word Quran audio for their examples, so each tajweed rule is heard in a real ayah.

## Left out on purpose

The book's lesson 3 (the Arabic names of 32 teeth) is left out: unit 11 names only the parts of the mouth that a learner needs to place a letter. The Qaida's repeated drill pages are not separate units: their rows form the practice pools, and the app's review brings back the items a learner gets wrong.
