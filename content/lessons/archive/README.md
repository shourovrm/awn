# Archived lessons

Files here are not built into the app. They stay for their wording, examples and clip ids, which a
later unit may reuse. Each set below records why it left, so the question is not reopened.

## Reading units of the Wikibooks and Noorani order (`en/letters`, `bn/letters`)

Nine units written on 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23: ba ta tha, letters by name, short vowels, tanween,
small long vowels, madd and leen, sukoon, shaddah, a whole ayah. They followed the Wikibooks
*Arabic* alphabet pages and the Noorani Qaida's order.

Decision (maintainer, 2026-11-10): the Reading track is the eleven units built on 2026-11-08 from
the Averroes Qaida, `letters` orders 1 to 11. These nine were renumbered to orders 12 to 20 on that
day instead of being removed, so the app showed them after unit 11 as if they were the course's
continuation. They are archived, not deleted, and do not come back: the Averroes plan continues
with Tajweed units (makharij review, the word Allah, kinds of madd, noon and meem sakin, heavy
and light raa, stopping and starting, stop signs), to be written as new files.

Progress made in these units is not carried over to the Averroes units, because the material and
the order differ. `LegacyLessonProgress` no longer maps their ids.
