## Screen 1: Tanween

Each of these ends in a doubled vowel mark: [[بًا]] is read ban, [[بٍ]] bin, [[بٌ]] bun. The doubled mark adds an n that is not written. It is called tanween: two fathas, two kasras or two dammas on the last letter of a word.

With an n written, [[بَنْ]] sounds the same as [[بًا]].

reveal: [[بَنْ]] ban; [[بًا]] ban; [[بِنْ]] bin; [[بٍ]] bin; [[بُنْ]] bun; [[بٌ]] bun

## Screen 2: Tanween on every letter

Two fathas usually come with an alif after them, as in [[بًا]]. The alif is not read.
