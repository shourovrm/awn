## Screen 1: Heavy and light sounds

Ten pairs of letters sound close to each other. Each pair differs in weight or in where it is made. Tap a pair to hear both.

pairs:
- [[ذ]] [[ظ]] | dhāl: light, soft · ẓā: heavy | qaida-01/009+qaida-01/017
- [[ك]] [[ق]] | kāf: light · qāf: heavy | qaida-01/022+qaida-01/021
- [[و]] [[ب]] | wāw: lips rounded · bā: lips closed | qaida-01/026+qaida-01/002
- [[د]] [[ض]] | dāl: tip of the tongue · ḍād: side of the tongue | qaida-01/008+qaida-01/015
- [[ء]] [[ع]] | hamzah: start of the throat · ʿayn: middle of the throat, air flows | qaida-01/028+qaida-01/018
- [[ت]] [[ط]] | tā: light · ṭā: heavy | qaida-01/003+qaida-01/016
- [[ه]] [[ح]] | hā: start of the throat · ḥā: middle of the throat, air flows | qaida-01/027+qaida-01/006
- [[ز]] [[ج]] | zāy: a whistle · jīm: no air flows | qaida-01/011+qaida-01/005
- [[ث]] [[س]] | thā: soft, tongue on the teeth · sīn: light, a hiss | qaida-01/004+qaida-01/012
- [[س]] [[ص]] | sīn: light · ṣād: heavy | qaida-01/012+qaida-01/014

tip: For a heavy letter the back of the tongue rises toward the roof of the mouth and the sound fills the mouth. A light letter keeps the tongue low.

tip: For [[ث]], [[ذ]] and [[ظ]] the tip of the tongue rests lightly between the front teeth.

tip: Teachers join the seven heavy letters into one phrase to remember them: [[خُصَّ ضَغْطٍ قِظْ]], khuṣṣa ḍaghṭin qiẓ.
