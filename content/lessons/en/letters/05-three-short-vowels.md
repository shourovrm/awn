## Screen 1: Fatha, kasra and damma

Three small marks give a letter a short vowel: fatha says a, kasra says i, damma says u. Together they are called harakah. A harakah is said quickly, never stretched.

groups:
- [[بَ]] [[تَ]] | Fatha: a stroke above, ba
- [[بِ]] [[تِ]] | Kasra: a stroke below, bi
- [[بُ]] [[تُ]] | Damma: a small wāw above, bu

Read [[بَ]] in three steps, as a teacher points:

- Point at the letter: bā
- Point at the mark: fatha
- Say both: ba

Alif itself takes no mark. A letter that looks like alif and carries a mark is hamzah: [[اَ]] [[اِ]] [[اُ]].
