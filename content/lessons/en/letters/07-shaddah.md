## Screen 1: A doubled letter

{{1:2:3}} is read rabbi, Lord. The [[ب]] carries a small mark like a w, and it is said twice: rab-bi.

That mark is the shaddah. A letter with a shaddah is read once with a sukoon and once with its vowel.

## Screen 2: Shaddah with a vowel

The shaddah sits with a vowel mark. The vowel tells how the second half is read.

- {{2:28:7}}: thum-ma, then
- {{1:5:1}}: iy-yā-ka, You alone
- {{108:1:1}}: in-nā, indeed We

- ra-bi
- rab-bi
- rā-bi
answer: 2

## Answer: rab-bi

The shaddah on [[ب]] doubles it: one b closes the first syllable, the second opens the next.

## Answer: The letter [[م]]

The [[م]] carries the shaddah: thum-ma.
