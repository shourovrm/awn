## Screen 1: Five areas of the mouth

mouth: throat tongue lips nose open_mouth

Every letter is made at one place in the mouth or throat, called its makhraj. The places fall into five areas.

- Throat, six letters: [[ء]] [[ه]] [[ع]] [[ح]] [[غ]] [[خ]]
- Tongue, eighteen letters: [[ق]] [[ك]] [[ج]] [[ش]] [[ي]] [[ض]] [[ل]] [[ن]] [[ر]] [[ط]] [[د]] [[ت]] [[ص]] [[س]] [[ز]] [[ظ]] [[ذ]] [[ث]]
- Lips, four letters: [[ف]] [[و]] [[ب]] [[م]]
- Open mouth, the long vowels: [[ا]] [[و]] [[ي]]
- Nose, ghunnah, the humming of [[ن]] and [[م]]

## Screen 2: The throat

mouth: throat

Six letters come from the throat, two from each part of it. The start of the throat is nearest the chest and the end is nearest the mouth. Tap a letter to hear it alone.

tip: A saying lists the throat letters in order by the first letter of each word: [[أَخِي هَاكَ عِلْمًا حَازَهُ غَيْرُ خَاسِرٍ]], akhī hāka ʿilman ḥāzahu ghayru khāsir.

## Screen 3: Back, middle and side of the tongue

mouth: tongue

Four places in the back, middle and side of the tongue make six letters. Tap a letter to hear it alone.

- Very back of the tongue, against the soft palate above it
- Back of the tongue, a little further forward than the first
- Middle of the tongue, against the hard palate
- One side of the tongue, against the upper back teeth

## Screen 4: Tip of the tongue

mouth: tongue

The tip of the tongue touches six places, from the gum to the teeth. Tap a letter to hear it alone.

- Edge of the tongue tip, against the gum above the front teeth
- Tip of the tongue, against the gum, just in front of the first
- Top side of the tongue tip, a little behind the second, against the gum
- Tip of the tongue, against the roots of the upper front teeth
- Tip of the tongue, near the lower front teeth, with the upper teeth close above
- Tip of the tongue, against the tips of the upper front teeth

## Screen 5: Lips

mouth: lips

The lower lip against the tips of the upper front teeth makes [[ف]]. Both lips make [[ب]], [[م]] and [[و]]: they close for [[ب]] and [[م]] and round for [[و]]. Tap a letter to hear it alone.

## Screen 7: Nose

mouth: nose

The nose carries the humming of [[ن]] and [[م]] with a shaddah, the mark that doubles a letter. The humming is called ghunnah. Tap a word to hear it.
