## Screen 1: Letters at the start of a surah

{{2:1:1}} is read alif lām mīm. It is not a word: each letter is read by its name, as in the alphabet.

29 surahs open with letters like these. The wavy line above [[ل]] and [[م]] means the long vowel in the letter's name is held for six counts.

## Screen 2: One letter

Three surahs open with a single letter, read by its name.

- {{38:1:1}}: ṣād
- {{50:1:1}}: qāf
- {{68:1:1}}: nūn

## Screen 3: Two to five letters

- {{20:1:1}}: ṭā hā
- {{36:1:1}}: yā sīn
- {{19:1:1}}: kāf hā yā ʿayn ṣād

## Check: Reading {{2:1:1}}

question: How is {{2:1:1}} read?
- a-la-ma
- alif lām mīm
- alam
answer: 2

## Answer: alif lām mīm

Each of the three letters is read by its name: [[ا]] alif, [[ل]] lām, [[م]] mīm.

## Check: One letter

question: How is {{50:1:1}} read?
- qa
- qu
- qāf
answer: 3

## Answer: qāf

The letter [[ق]] is read by its name, qāf.
