## Screen 1: Al-Ikhlas, ayah 1

{{112:1}}

This ayah has four words. Each one uses a mark from the units before.

## Screen 2: Word by word

- {{112:1:1}}: qul, say; sukoon on [[ل]]
- {{112:1:2}}: huwa, He; damma then fatha
- {{112:1:3}}: Allāh; shaddah on [[ل]]
- {{112:1:4}}: aḥadun, One; tanween at the end

The name Allāh is read with a long ā after the doubled [[ل]]. Learn it as a whole word for now; the Tajweed track explains its rules.

## Screen 3: Ayah 3

{{112:3:1}} {{112:3:2}} is read lam yalid, He did not beget. Both words end in a sukoon.

## Check: The mark on {{112:1:1}}

question: Why is {{112:1:1}} read qul and not qula?
- The [[ل]] has a sukoon
- The [[ل]] has a shaddah
answer: 1

## Answer: Sukoon

The sukoon on [[ل]] closes the word with no vowel.

## Check: The ending of {{112:1:4}}

question: How does {{112:1:4}} end?
- -ad
- -adun
- -adā
answer: 2

## Answer: Tanween

Two dammas on [[د]] are read un: aḥadun.
