## Screen 1: The shaddah mark

The small w-shaped mark [[ّ]] is the shaddah. It doubles a letter: it is read once with a sukun and once with its vowel. [[أَبْ]] and [[بَ]] together make [[أَبَّ]], abba.

reveal: [[أَحَّ]] aḥḥa; [[إِحَّ]] iḥḥa; [[أُحَّ]] uḥḥa; [[أَخَّ]] akhkha; [[إِخَّ]] ikhkha; [[أُخَّ]] ukhkha; [[أَدَّ]] adda; [[إِدَّ]] idda; [[أُدَّ]] udda; [[أَذَّ]] adhdha; [[إِذَّ]] idhdha; [[أُذَّ]] udhdha; [[أَرَّ]] arra; [[إِرَّ]] irra; [[أُرَّ]] urra; [[أَزَّ]] azza; [[إِزَّ]] izza; [[أُزَّ]] uzza; [[أَسَّ]] assa; [[إِسَّ]] issa; [[أُسَّ]] ussa; [[أَشَّ]] ashsha; [[إِشَّ]] ishsha; [[أُشَّ]] ushsha; [[أَصَّ]] aṣṣa; [[إِصَّ]] iṣṣa; [[أُصَّ]] uṣṣa; [[أَضَّ]] aḍḍa; [[إِضَّ]] iḍḍa; [[أُضَّ]] uḍḍa; [[أَظَّ]] aẓẓa; [[إِظَّ]] iẓẓa; [[أُظَّ]] uẓẓa; [[أَعَّ]] aʿʿa; [[إِعَّ]] iʿʿa; [[أُعَّ]] uʿʿa; [[أَغَّ]] aghgha; [[إِغَّ]] ighgha; [[أُغَّ]] ughgha; [[أَفَّ]] affa; [[إِفَّ]] iffa; [[أُفَّ]] uffa; [[أَقَّ]] aqqa; [[إِقَّ]] iqqa; [[أُقَّ]] uqqa; [[أَكَّ]] akka; [[إِكَّ]] ikka; [[أُكَّ]] ukka; [[أَلَّ]] alla; [[إِلَّ]] illa; [[أُلَّ]] ulla; [[أَمَّ]] amma; [[إِمَّ]] imma; [[أُمَّ]] umma; [[أَنَّ]] anna; [[إِنَّ]] inna; [[أُنَّ]] unna; [[أَوَّ]] awwa; [[إِوَّ]] iwwa; [[أُوَّ]] uwwa; [[أَهَّ]] ahha; [[إِهَّ]] ihha; [[أُهَّ]] uhha; [[أَيَّ]] ayya; [[إِيَّ]] iyya; [[أُيَّ]] uyya

## Screen 2: Words with shaddah

## Screen 3: Letters that are not read before a shaddah

A shaddah needs a vowel on the letter before it. After [[ال]], the lām is not read and the next letter takes the shaddah: [[وَالصَّيْفِ]] is waṣ-ṣayfi.

reveal: [[وَالتِّيْنِ]] wat-tīni; [[اِلَّا الَّذِيْنَ]] illal-ladhīna; [[وَاللَّيْلِ]] wal-layli

words:
- {{86:2:3}} {{86:2:4}} | māṭ-ṭāriqu
- {{17:11:3}} | bish-sharri
- {{20:7:5}} {{20:7:6}} | yaʿlamus-sirra

A letter with sukun right before a shaddah is not read either: in [[إِذ ذَّهَبَ]] the first [[ذ]] merges into the second.

reveal: [[اَحَطْتُّ]] aḥaṭṭu; [[مَا عَبَدْتُّمْ]] mā ʿabattum; [[مَا وَعَدْتُّنَا]] mā waʿattunā; [[نَجْعَلْ لَّهُ]] najʿal-lahu; [[يَجْعَلْ لَّكُمْ]] yajʿal-lakum

## Screen 4: Ghunnah on nūn and mīm

When [[ن]] or [[م]] has a shaddah, it is held in the nose for two counts. This humming is called ghunnah: [[إِنَّ]], [[ثُمَّ]].

reveal: [[لَمَّ]] lamma; [[خُنَّسْ]] khunnas; [[كُنَّسْ]] kunnas; [[ظَنَّنَا]] ẓannanā; [[لَهَمَّتْ]] lahammat; [[قُلْ اِنِّيْ]] qul innī
