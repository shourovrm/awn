## Screen 1: Hamzah to thā

Each letter has twelve readings: the three vowels alone, then two syllables of the same letter with every mix of vowels. [[بَبُ]] is read babu: ba, then bu. Read from the right.

Hamzah comes first. It takes its vowels like any other letter.
