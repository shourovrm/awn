## Screen 1: Four forms of a letter

A letter changes shape with its place in a word. Alone and at the end, [[ب]] is a full bowl; at the start and in the middle it shrinks to a short tooth, and its dot stays.

Six letters, [[ا]] [[د]] [[ذ]] [[ر]] [[ز]] [[و]], never join the letter after them, so they have two forms only. Tap a form to hear the letter.

## Screen 4: Lām to yā, and hamzah

[[و]] never joins the next letter; [[ل]], [[م]], [[ن]], [[ه]] and [[ي]] do. [[ه]] changes shape the most.

Hamzah stands alone or sits on a seat: on alif at the start, on a yā tooth in the middle and at the end.
