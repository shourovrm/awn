## Screen 1: The letter [[ب]] in four shapes

[[ب]] is the letter bā. It sounds like b in book and has one dot below.

A letter changes shape with its place in a word. [[ب]] has four shapes: alone [[ب]], at the start [[بـ]], in the middle [[ـبـ]] and at the end [[ـب]].

## Screen 2: [[ب]] in Quran words

Each shape of [[ب]] appears in a real word.

- Start: {{1:1:1}} (1:1:1), in the name
- Middle: {{6:112:4}} (6:112:4), Prophet
- End: {{2:2:2}} (2:2:2), the book
- Alone: {{1:2:3}} (1:2:3), Lord

In {{1:2:3}} the letter before [[ب]] is [[ر]]. The letter [[ر]] does not join to the letter after it, so [[ب]] stands alone.

## Screen 3: [[ب]], [[ت]] and [[ث]]

[[ب]], [[ت]] and [[ث]] have the same body. Only the dots differ: [[ب]] has one dot below, [[ت]] has two above and [[ث]] has three above.

- One dot below: {{1:1:1}} (1:1:1), in the name
- Two dots above: {{2:255:9}} (2:255:9), overtakes Him
- Three dots above: {{2:28:7}} (2:28:7), then

## Screen 4: Letters that do not join forward

Six letters join to the letter before them but never to the letter after them: [[ا]], [[د]], [[ذ]], [[ر]], [[ز]] and [[و]]. The letter after one of them starts fresh, in its start shape or alone.

In {{108:1:3}} (108:1:3), Al-Kawthar, the [[و]] does not join forward, so the [[ث]] after it takes its start shape.

## Check: A letter that does not join

question: Which letter never joins to the letter after it?
- [[ب]]
- [[ر]]
- [[ت]]
answer: 2

## Answer: The letter [[ر]]

[[ر]] joins only to the letter before it. That is why [[ب]] stands alone in {{1:2:3}}.
