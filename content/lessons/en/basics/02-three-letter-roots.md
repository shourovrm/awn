## Screen 1: Three-letter roots

A root is the three letters that a family of words shares. The root [[ك ت ب]], which carries the idea of writing, sits in three words from the Quran.

- {{2:2:2}}, the book (2:2)
- {{2:183:4}}, is prescribed (2:183)
- {{2:79:3}}, they write (2:79)

Most Arabic verbs have a root of three letters. The root gives the idea. The vowels and extra letters around it give the exact meaning, so {{2:183:4}} is passive and {{2:79:3}} means they write.
