## Screen 1: Attached words

[[بِ]] and [[وَ]] are one-letter words written as the first letter of the next word. [[بِ]] means in or by, and [[وَ]] means and.

- {{1:1:1}}, in (the) name: [[بِ]] plus [[سْمِ]] (1:1)
- {{2:3:3}}, in the unseen: [[بِ]] plus [[ٱلْغَيْبِ]] (2:3)
- {{2:3:4}}, and establish: [[وَ]] plus [[يُقِيمُونَ]] (2:3)
