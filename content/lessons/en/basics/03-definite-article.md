## Screen 1: The word [[ال]]

[[ال]] at the start of a word means the. {{2:2:2}} is the book, from [[كتاب]], book.

In {{1:2:1}}, the praise, [[ال]] is pronounced al. In {{1:1:3}}, the Most Gracious, the [[ل]] is not pronounced, because it blends into [[ر]], a letter said with the tip of the tongue. The [[ل]] is still printed.
