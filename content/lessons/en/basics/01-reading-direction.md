## Screen 1: Reading direction

Arabic is read from right to left, so the first word of an ayah is the one on the right.

The first ayah of the Quran has four words. {{1:1:1}}, In (the) name, is on the right. {{1:1:4}}, the Most Merciful, is on the left.
