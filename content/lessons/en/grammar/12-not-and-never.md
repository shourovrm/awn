## Screen 1: No doubt

{{2:2:3}} {{2:2:4}}, lā rayba, means there is no doubt. Arabic has several words for not, and each fits a different kind of sentence.

words: ending
- {{2:2:3}} {{2:2:4}} | lā rayba · no doubt | 1-2
- {{2:8:9}} {{2:8:10}} | wa-mā hum · and they are not | 2-3
- {{112:3:1}} {{112:3:2}} | lam yalid · He did not beget | 1-2
- {{2:24:4}} {{2:24:5}} | wa-lan tafʿalū · and you will never do it | 2-3
- {{70:2:2}} | laysa · is not | 1-3

## Screen 2: Which word for not

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [[لَا]] | no, not; with a present verb, does not |
| [[مَا]] | not, before a noun or a past verb |
| [[لَمْ]] | did not, with a present-form verb |
| [[لَنْ]] | will never |
| [[لَيْسَ]] | is not |

## Check: Lam yalid

question: What does {{112:3:1}} {{112:3:2}} mean?
- He did not beget
- He will never beget
- He does not beget
answer: 1

## Answer: Did not

[[لَمْ]] before a present-form verb means did not.
