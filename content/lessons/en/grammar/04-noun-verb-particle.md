## Screen 1: Three kinds of word

{{2:11:6}} {{2:11:7}} means in the earth. It holds two kinds of word: {{2:11:7}}, the earth, names a thing, and {{2:11:6}}, in, links it to the rest of the sentence.

Every Arabic word is one of three kinds. A noun (ism) names a person, a thing or an idea. A verb (fiʿl) names an action and when it happens. A particle (ḥarf) has no full meaning alone; it links or shapes the words around it.

words:
- {{2:11:7}} | al-arḍ · the earth · noun
- {{2:8:2}} | an-nās · the people · noun
- {{2:30:2}} | qāla · he said · verb
- {{2:3:2}} | yuʾminūna · they believe · verb
- {{2:10:1}} | fī · in · particle
- {{2:5:2}} | ʿalā · on · particle

## Screen 2: Signs of a noun

{{2:11:7}} starts with [[ٱلْ]], the word for the. Only a noun takes [[ٱلْ]]. A noun can also end in tanween, a doubled vowel sign read -un, -an or -in, as in {{2:7:12}}, ʿaẓīmun, great.

words: ending
- {{2:11:7}} | al-arḍ · the earth | 1-2
- {{2:8:2}} | an-nās · the people | 1-2
- {{2:7:12}} | ʿaẓīmun · great | 4
- {{112:1:4}} | aḥadun · one | 3

## Answer: A verb names an action

{{2:30:2}}, qāla, means he said: an action that is finished. {{2:10:1}} is a particle and {{2:11:7}} is a noun.
