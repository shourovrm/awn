## Screen 1: He said, they said

{{2:30:2}}, qāla, means he said. A past-tense verb names a finished action. Its plain form means he did it, and an ending says who else did it.

words: ending
- {{2:30:2}} | qāla · he said
- {{66:11:9}} | qālat · she said | 4
- {{2:25:19}} | qālū · they said | 4-5
- {{77:43:5}} | kuntum · you were, a group | 3-4
- {{21:69:1}} | qulnā · We said | 3-4
- {{5:117:2}} | qultu · I said | 3
