## Screen 1: Words for people

{{112:1:2}} {{112:1:3}} means He is Allah. The first word, {{112:1:2}}, huwa, is a pronoun: a word that stands for someone already known. Arabic has one pronoun for a man, another for a woman and another for a group.

words:
- {{2:29:1}} | huwa · he
- {{97:5:2}} | hiya · she
- {{2:4:11}} | hum · they
- {{88:21:3}} | anta · you, one man
- {{109:3:2}} | antum · you, a group
- {{109:4:2}} | anā · I
- {{76:23:2}} | naḥnu · we

## Screen 2: No word for is

Arabic needs no word for is. A pronoun followed by a noun is already a whole sentence: {{2:5:7}} {{2:5:8}} means they are the successful.

words:
- {{112:1:2}} {{112:1:3}} | huwa llāhu · He is Allah
- {{2:5:7}} {{2:5:8}} | humu l-mufliḥūn · they are the successful

## Check: We

question: Which word means we?
- {{2:4:11}}
- {{76:23:2}}
- {{109:4:2}}
answer: 2

## Answer: Naḥnu

{{76:23:2}}, naḥnu, means we. {{109:4:2}} means I and {{2:4:11}} means they.
