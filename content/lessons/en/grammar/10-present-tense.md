## Screen 1: We worship, they believe

{{1:5:2}}, naʿbudu, means we worship. A present-tense verb names an action going on now or done as a habit. A letter in front of the root says who does it: [[يَ]] he or they, [[تَ]] you or she, [[نَ]] we, [[أَ]] I. The ending [[ـُونَ]] marks a group.

words: ending
- {{2:8:4}} | yaqūlu · he says | 1
- {{100:9:2}} | yaʿlamu · he knows | 1
- {{109:2:4}} | taʿbudūna · you worship, a group | 1, 5-6
- {{1:5:2}} | naʿbudu · we worship | 1
- {{109:2:2}} | aʿbudu · I worship | 1
- {{2:3:2}} | yuʾminūna · they believe | 1, 5-6

## Check: Who does it

question: Who does the action in {{1:5:2}}?

answer: 2

## Answer: We

The [[نَ]] in front of {{1:5:2}} means we.
