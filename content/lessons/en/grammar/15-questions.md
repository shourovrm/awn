## Screen 1: Question words

{{88:1:1}} {{88:1:2}}, hal atāka, means has there come to you. A question word at the start turns a sentence into a question. [[هَلْ]] and [[أَ]] ask yes or no; the others ask how, where, what or who.

words: ending
- {{88:1:1}} | hal · yes or no
- {{105:1:1}} {{105:1:2}} | a-lam tara · have you not seen | 1
- {{105:1:3}} | kayfa · how
- {{75:10:4}} | ayna · where
- {{101:2:1}} | mā · what
- {{2:255:20}} | man · who
