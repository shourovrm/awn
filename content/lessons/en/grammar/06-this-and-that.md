## Screen 1: Pointing words

{{2:25:20}}, hādhā, means this. It points at something, so it is a demonstrative. Arabic chooses the form by near or far, and by masculine, feminine or a group.

words:
- {{2:25:20}} | hādhā · this, masculine
- {{52:14:1}} | hādhihi · this, feminine
- {{2:2:1}} | dhālika · that, masculine
- {{79:12:2}} | tilka · that, feminine
- {{2:5:1}} | ulāʾika · those

## Check: That, feminine

question: Which word means that, for something feminine?
- {{52:14:1}}
- {{79:12:2}}
- {{2:2:1}}
answer: 2

## Answer: Tilka

{{79:12:2}}, tilka, points far and is feminine. {{52:14:1}} points near, and {{2:2:1}} is masculine.
