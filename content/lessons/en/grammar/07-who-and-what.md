## Screen 1: Words that join a description

{{1:7:2}}, alladhīna, in Al-Fatihah means those who: the path of those whom You have favoured. It is a relative pronoun, a word that joins a description to the people or things it describes.

words:
- {{2:17:3}} | alladhī · who, which · one
- {{1:7:2}} | alladhīna · who · a group
- {{2:8:3}} | man · whoever, who
- {{2:17:8}} | mā · what, whatever

## Screen 2: In a question

In Ayat al-Kursi, {{2:255:20}} {{2:255:21}} {{2:255:22}} {{2:255:23}} means who is it that intercedes. {{2:255:20}} asks who, and {{2:255:22}} joins the description, intercedes.

words:
- {{2:255:20}} {{2:255:21}} | man dhā · who is it
- {{2:255:22}} {{2:255:23}} | alladhī yashfaʿu · who intercedes

## Check: A group

question: Which word is used for a group of people?
- {{2:17:3}}
- {{1:7:2}}
- {{2:17:8}}
answer: 2

## Answer: Alladhīna

{{1:7:2}}, alladhīna, is for a group. {{2:17:3}} is for one, and {{2:17:8}} means what.

## Practice: Relative word

question: What does {{2:17:8}} mean?
- what
- who, a group
- those
answer: 1
explain: {{2:17:8}}, mā, means what or whatever.
