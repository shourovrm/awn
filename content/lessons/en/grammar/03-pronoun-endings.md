## Screen 1: The ending [[هُمْ]]

Ayah 2:3 has the word {{2:3:7}}.

{{2:3}}

{{2:3:7}} is one word made of two parts.

[[رَزَقْنَا]] (we provided) + [[هُمْ]] (them) = {{2:3:7}} (We have provided them)

The first part, [[رَزَقْنَا]], is a verb. A verb is a word for an action, and [[رَزَقْنَا]] means we provided.

The second part, [[هُمْ]], is an ending. An ending is a letter or letters joined to the end of a word. This ending means them.

An ending can join to a noun as well. A noun is a word for a person or a thing. In {{2:217:48}}, 2:217, the noun [[أَعْمَـٰلُ]] means deeds, and the ending [[هُمْ]] makes it their deeds.

[[أَعْمَـٰلُ]] (deeds) + [[هُمْ]] (them) = {{2:217:48}} (their deeds)

## Screen 2: Pronoun endings

The ending [[هُ]] means him after a verb and his after a noun. The other endings work the same way.

| Ending | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| [[هُ]] | him, his | {{2:255:37}} (2:255:37), his knowledge |
| [[هُمْ]] | them, their | {{2:3:7}} (2:3:7), We have provided them |
| [[كَ]] | you, one person | {{2:4:5}} (2:4:5), to you |
| [[كُمْ]] | you, more than one | {{2:21:4}} (2:21:4), your Lord |
| [[نَا]] | us, our | {{2:285:25}} (2:285:25), our Lord |

After the vowel [[ـِ]], or after [[ـِي]] or [[ـيْ]], the vowel of [[هُمْ]] changes to [[ـِ]]. For this reason {{2:79:20}}, their hands, 2:79, ends in [[هِمْ]].

## Answer: The ending [[كُمْ]]

The ending [[كُمْ]] means you, for more than one person. {{2:21:6}} means he created you.
