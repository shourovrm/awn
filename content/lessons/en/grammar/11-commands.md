## Screen 1: Say, read, guide us

{{2:80:8}}, qul, means say. A command tells someone to act. Many commands start with a joining alif, [[ٱ]], read i or u when you start with the word; the ending says who is told: [[ـُوا]] a group, [[ـِى]] a woman.

words: ending
- {{2:80:8}} | qul · say
- {{96:1:1}} | iqraʾ · read | 1
- {{1:6:1}} | ihdinā · guide us | 1
- {{71:3:2}} | uʿbudū · worship, a group | 1, 5-6
- {{89:28:1}} | irjiʿī · return, a woman | 1, 5
- {{79:17:1}} | idhhab · go | 1

## Screen 2: Do not

[[لَا]] before a present-tense verb turns it into a ban: {{2:22:18}} {{2:22:19}}, fa-lā tajʿalū, means so do not make.

words: ending
- {{2:22:18}} {{2:22:19}} | fa-lā tajʿalū · so do not make | 2-3

## Check: Who is told

question: Who is told to act in {{71:3:2}}?
- one man
- a group
- one woman
answer: 2

## Answer: A group

The ending [[ـُوا]] of {{71:3:2}} speaks to a group.
