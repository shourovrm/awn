## Screen 1: Words of place and direction

{{2:11:6}} {{2:11:7}} means in the earth. {{2:11:6}}, fī, is a preposition: a particle that places a noun in space or time. The noun after a preposition ends in an i sound, the kasra, as the [[ض]] of {{2:11:7}} does.

words: ending
- {{2:11:6}} {{2:11:7}} | fi l-arḍi · in the earth | 7
- {{2:4:8}} {{2:4:9}} | min qablika · from before you | 5
- {{96:8:2}} {{96:8:3}} | ilā rabbika · to your Lord | 5
- {{107:5:3}} {{107:5:4}} | ʿan ṣalātihim · from their prayer | 6

## Screen 2: Prepositions joined to the word

Some prepositions are one letter written on the front of the noun: [[بِ]] in or with, [[لِ]] for, [[كَ]] like. {{1:2:2}}, lillāhi, is li, for, joined to Allah.

words: ending
- {{1:2:2}} | lillāhi · for Allah | 1
- {{2:8:6}} | billāhi · in Allah | 1
- {{1:1:1}} | bismi · in the name | 1

## Answer: In

{{2:11:6}}, fī, means in. The noun after it, {{2:11:7}}, ends in a kasra.
