## Screen 1: Two of a thing

{{28:15:10}}, rajulayni, means two men. An Arabic noun shows whether there is one, two, or more than two. The ending [[ـَيْنِ]] or [[ـَانِ]] makes two of a thing; this form is the dual.

words: ending
- {{28:15:10}} | rajulayni · two men | 4-5
- {{36:14:4}} | ithnayni · two | 4-5
- {{78:40:10}} | yadāhu · his two hands | 3

## Screen 2: More than two

The ending [[ـُونَ]] or [[ـِينَ]] makes a group of men, and [[ـَاتِ]] a group of women. Many nouns change inside instead: {{2:7:4}}, qulūbihim, their hearts, comes from [[قَلْب]], heart.

words: ending
- {{109:1:3}} | al-kāfirūna · the disbelievers | 6-7
- {{85:10:4}} | al-muʾminīna · the believing men | 7-8
- {{85:10:5}} | wal-muʾmināti · and the believing women | 7-8
- {{2:7:4}} | qulūbihim · their hearts

## Check: How many

question: How many men are meant in {{28:15:10}}?
- one
- two
- more than two
answer: 2

## Answer: Two

The ending [[ـَيْنِ]] makes the dual: two men.
