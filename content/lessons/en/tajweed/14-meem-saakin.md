## Screen 1: Three ways to read a silent meem

A meem saakin is a [[م]] with no vowel, as at the end of {{1:7:4}}, ʿalayhim. The letter after it decides how it is read.

- Merged before [[م]]: the two m sounds become one doubled m, held through the nose for two counts. This is idgham shafawi.
- Hidden before [[ب]]: the lips close lightly and the nasal sound is held for two counts. This is ikhfa shafawi.
- Clear before every other letter, with no hold. This is izhar shafawi.

## Screen 2: Merged before meem

The meem at the end of the first word joins the doubled meem that starts the next. Hold the one m through the nose for two counts.

## Screen 3: Hidden before baa

Close the lips lightly for the m, keep the nasal sound for two counts, then open them for the [[ب]].

tip: Do not press the lips hard. A hard press turns the hidden m into a clear m.

## Screen 4: Keep the clear meem short

A clear meem has no nasal hold. Take extra care before [[و]] and [[ف]], which are also lip letters: the meem is still said cleanly and not hidden.

## Answer: Hidden

Before [[ب]] the meem is hidden and held for two counts.
