## Screen 1: Three ways to read a silent meem

A meem saakin is a [[م]] with no vowel. The letter after it decides how it is read.

- Merged before [[م]]: the two m sounds become one doubled m, held through the nose for two counts. {{2:10:2}}, qulūbihim-maraḍ.
- Hidden before [[ب]]: the lips close lightly and the nasal sound is held for two counts. {{2:8:10}}, hum-bi-muʾminīn.
- Clear before every other letter: {{1:7:4}}, ʿalayhim ghayri.

## Screen 2: Keep the clear meem short

A clear meem has no nasal hold. Take extra care before [[و]] and [[ف]], which are also lip letters: the meem is still said cleanly and not hidden.

{{2:3:7}}, razaqnāhum yunfiqūn: the meem before [[ي]] is clear.

## Answer: Hidden

Before [[ب]] the meem is hidden and held for two counts.
