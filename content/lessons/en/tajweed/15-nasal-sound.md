## Screen 1: Noon and meem with a shaddah

A [[ن]] or [[م]] with a shaddah is held through the nose for two counts. That held nasal sound is the ghunnah.

words: ghunnah
- {{2:6:1}} | inna | 2
- {{114:1:4}} | an-nās | 3
- {{2:17:6}} | falammā | 3
- {{78:1:1}} | ʿamma | 2
- {{108:1:1}} | innā | 2
- {{2:3:6}} | wa-mimmā | 3

tip: Pinch your nose while saying inna. The sound stops, because it comes through the nose.

## Check: Length

question: How long is the nasal sound in {{2:6:1}}?
- One count
- Two counts
- Six counts
answer: 2

## Answer: Two counts

A [[ن]] or [[م]] with a shaddah is held for two counts.
