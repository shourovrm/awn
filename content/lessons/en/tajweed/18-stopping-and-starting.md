## Screen 1: Stopping on a word

To stop on a word, drop its last vowel and give the last letter a sukoon.

words:
- {{112:1:4}} | aḥadun → aḥad | 3
- {{113:1:4}} | al-falaqi → al-falaq | 5
- {{114:1:4}} | an-nāsi → an-nās | 5
- {{1:2:4}} | al-ʿālamīna → al-ʿālamīn | 7
- {{112:2:2}} | aṣ-ṣamadu → aṣ-ṣamad | 5
- {{96:2:4}} | ʿalaqin → ʿalaq | 3

## Screen 2: Endings that change at a stop

Two fathas on the last letter become a long ā at a stop:

words:
- {{78:13:3}} | wahhājan → wahhājā | 4-5
- {{100:1:2}} | ḍabḥan → ḍabḥā | 3-4
- {{100:3:2}} | ṣubḥan → ṣubḥā | 3-4

A round taa, [[ة]], becomes h at a stop:

words:
- {{2:7:9}} | ghishāwatun → ghishāwah | 4
- {{101:1:1}} | al-qāriʿatu → al-qāriʿah | 7
- {{101:11:2}} | ḥāmiyatun → ḥāmiyah | 5

A word that already ends in a sukoon or a long vowel stays as it is.

## Screen 3: The joining alif

Many words start with an alif that carries a small mark like a ṣ, [[ٱ]]. It is a joining alif, hamzat al-waṣl: in the middle of reading it is silent and the word joins the one before. It is read only when you start with that word.

- Before the l of "al": start with a.
- Otherwise, look at the third letter. If it carries a damma, start with u. If not, start with i.
- A few nouns always start with i, whatever their third letter: [[ٱسْم]] ism, [[ٱبْن]] ibn, [[ٱمْرَأَة]] imraʾah.

words:
- {{1:2:1}} | start: al-ḥamdu | 1
- {{2:58:3}} | start: udkhulū | 1
- {{1:6:1}} | start: ihdinā | 1
- {{96:1:1}} | start: iqraʾ | 1
- {{87:1:2}} | start: isma | 1
- {{73:3:3}} | start: unquṣ | 1
