## Screen 1: Seven heavy letters

Seven letters are always heavy: [[خ]] [[ص]] [[ض]] [[غ]] [[ط]] [[ق]] [[ظ]]. A heavy letter is said with the back of the tongue raised, so the mouth fills and the sound is full. Every other letter is light, said with the tongue low.

The seven are often learned as one phrase, [[خُصَّ ضَغْطٍ قِظْ]].

words: heavy
- {{79:40:3}} | khāfa | 1
- {{112:2:2}} | aṣ-ṣamad | 3
- {{1:7:9}} | aḍ-ḍāllīn | 3
- {{79:37:3}} | ṭaghā | 1-2
- {{113:1:1}} | qul | 1
- {{2:7:12}} | ʿaẓīm | 2

## Screen 2: Alif follows the letter before it

An alif has no weight of its own. After a heavy letter it is heavy:

words: heavy
- {{71:2:1}} | qāla | 1-2
- {{79:40:3}} | khāfa | 1-2
- {{81:22:2}} | ṣāḥibukum | 1-2

After a light letter it is light:

words:
- {{70:4:7}} | kāna | 1-2
- {{101:11:1}} | nārun | 1-2
- {{109:4:3}} | ʿābidun | 1-2

## Screen 3: The l in Allah

The l in the name Allah is heavy after a fatha or a damma, and at the start of reading:

words: heavy
- {{2:7:1}} {{2:7:2}} | khatama-llāhu | 5-6
- {{112:1:2}} {{112:1:3}} | huwa-llāhu | 4-5
- {{3:55:2}} {{3:55:3}} | qāla-llāhu | 5-6
- {{112:2:1}} | allāhu | 2-3

It is light after a kasra:

words:
- {{1:1:1}} {{1:1:2}} | bismi-llāhi | 5-6
- {{2:8:6}} | billāhi | 3-4
- {{1:2:2}} | lillāhi | 1-2

## Screen 4: Raa

[[ر]] is heavy when it carries a fatha or a damma:

words: heavy
- {{1:2:3}} | rabbi | 1
- {{1:1:3}} | ar-raḥmān | 3
- {{110:1:3}} | naṣru | 3
- {{108:2:3}} | wanḥar | 5

It is light when it carries a kasra:

words:
- {{78:40:4}} | qarīban | 2
- {{106:2:2}} | riḥlata | 1
- {{101:1:1}} | al-qāriʿah | 5
- {{114:5:4}} | ṣudūri | 4

A [[ر]] with a sukoon takes the weight of the vowel before it: after a fatha or damma it is heavy, as in wanḥar, and after a kasra it is light.

- [[ق]]
- [[ك]]
- [[ت]]
answer: 1

## Answer: [[ق]]

[[ق]] is one of the seven: [[خ]] [[ص]] [[ض]] [[غ]] [[ط]] [[ق]] [[ظ]].

## Check: Allah after a kasra

question: How is the l in Allah read in {{1:1:2}}, after bismi?
- Heavy
- Light
answer: 2

## Answer: Light

The m of bismi carries a kasra, so the l in Allah is light.
