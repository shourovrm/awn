## Screen 1: Five areas

mouth: none

Every letter is made in one of five areas: the throat, the tongue, the lips, the nose and the open space of the mouth. The exact spot is the letter's makhraj, its point of articulation. The 29 letters, counting hamza, come from 17 such points.

Knowing the area tells you how to say a letter you hear wrongly. [[ح]] and [[ه]] are both h sounds, but [[ح]] is made in the middle of the throat and [[ه]] at its bottom.

## Screen 2: The throat

mouth: throat

Six letters come from the throat, in three pairs from the bottom up:

- Bottom, nearest the chest: [[ء]] and [[ه]]
- Middle: [[ع]] and [[ح]]
- Top, nearest the mouth: [[غ]] and [[خ]]

words:
- {{112:1:4}} | aḥad · hamza, ḥāʾ | 1-2
- {{112:1:2}} | huwa · hāʾ | 1
- {{1:2:4}} | al-ʿālamīn · ʿayn | 3
- {{1:2:1}} | al-ḥamdu · ḥāʾ | 3
- {{1:7:5}} | ghayri · ghayn | 1
- {{79:40:3}} | khāfa · khāʾ | 1

tip: Say each word with a hand on your throat. The sound starts lower for [[ء]] and [[ه]] than for [[غ]] and [[خ]].

## Screen 3: The tongue

mouth: tongue

Eighteen letters are made with the tongue against the roof of the mouth or the teeth.

- Back of the tongue: [[ق]] (furthest back) and [[ك]]
- Middle of the tongue: [[ج]] [[ش]] [[ي]]
- Side of the tongue against the upper back teeth: [[ض]]
- Edge of the tongue against the gums: [[ل]] [[ن]] [[ر]]
- Tip against the roots of the upper front teeth: [[ت]] [[د]] [[ط]]
- Tip against the edge of the upper front teeth: [[ث]] [[ذ]] [[ظ]]
- Tip near the lower front teeth, with a whistle: [[ص]] [[س]] [[ز]]

## Screen 4: Lips, nose and the open mouth

mouth: lips nose open_mouth

- Lower lip against the tips of the upper front teeth: [[ف]]
- Both lips: [[ب]] (moist part), [[م]] (dry part) and [[و]] (lips rounded, not closed)
- The nose carries the nasal sound of [[ن]] and [[م]], the ghunnah.
- The open space of the mouth carries the long vowels: [[ا]] after a fatha, [[و]] after a damma and [[ي]] after a kasra.

words:
- {{113:1:4}} | al-falaq · fāʾ | 3
- {{1:1:1}} | bismi · bāʾ, mīm | 1, 3
- {{112:1:2}} | huwa · wāw | 2
- {{2:6:1}} | inna · nasal nūn | 2
- {{2:8:4}} | yaqūlu · long ū | 3
- {{2:11:2}} | qīla · long ī | 2

## Screen 5: Letters that sound alike

Pairs that sound alike to a beginner come from different points or differ in weight:

- [[ت]] and [[ط]], [[س]] and [[ص]], [[د]] and [[ض]], [[ذ]] and [[ظ]]: the second of each pair is heavy, said with the back of the tongue raised.
- [[ه]] and [[ح]], [[ء]] and [[ع]]: the first of each pair is lower in the throat.
- [[ك]] and [[ق]]: [[ق]] is further back.

## Check: The throat

question: Where is [[ع]] made?
- The lips
- The middle of the throat
- The tip of the tongue
answer: 2

## Answer: Middle of the throat

[[ع]] and [[ح]] share the middle of the throat.

## Check: The lips

question: Which letter is made with the lower lip and the upper teeth?
- [[ف]]
- [[ب]]
- [[م]]
answer: 1

## Answer: [[ف]]

[[ب]] and [[م]] close both lips; [[ف]] touches the lower lip to the upper teeth.

## Practice: Throat letter

question: Which of these is a throat letter?

- [[ك]]
- [[ج]]
answer: 1
explain: [[خ]] comes from the top of the throat.

## Practice: Back of the tongue

- [[ج]]

## Practice: Two h sounds

question: Which h is made lower in the throat?
- [[ه]]
- [[ح]]
answer: 1
explain: [[ه]] is at the bottom of the throat, [[ح]] in the middle.

## Practice: Heavy s

question: Which s is heavy?
- [[س]]
- [[ص]]
answer: 2
explain: [[ص]] is said with the back of the tongue raised.

## Practice: Heavy t

question: Which t is heavy?

- [[ت]]

## Practice: Area of a letter

question: Where is [[م]] made?
- The throat
- The lips
- The middle of the tongue
answer: 2
explain: [[م]] closes the dry part of both lips.

## Practice: Area of a letter

question: Where is [[ش]] made?
- The middle of the tongue
- The lips
- The bottom of the throat
answer: 1
explain: [[ش]] shares the middle of the tongue with [[ج]] and [[ي]].

## Practice: The side of the tongue

question: Which letter is made with the side of the tongue?
- [[د]]
- [[ض]]
- [[ذ]]
answer: 2
explain: [[ض]] is the only letter made with the side of the tongue.

## Practice: Whistling letters

question: Which letters are said with a whistle?
- [[ص]] [[س]] [[ز]]
- [[ث]] [[ذ]] [[ظ]]
- [[ق]] [[ك]] [[ج]]
answer: 1
explain: [[ص]] [[س]] [[ز]] are made at the tip of the tongue near the lower front teeth.

## Practice: Throat pair

question: Which letter is made at the same point as [[ء]]?
- [[ع]]
- [[ه]]
- [[غ]]
answer: 2
explain: [[ء]] and [[ه]] both come from the bottom of the throat.

## Practice: Open mouth

question: Which sound comes from the open space of the mouth?
- The long ā in {{2:30:2}}
- The [[ق]] in {{2:30:2}}
answer: 1
explain: Long vowels have no fixed point; they flow from the open mouth.
