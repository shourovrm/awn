## Screen 1: Six signs in the Madani Mushaf

Small letters above the line tell you where to stop. They are guidance for meaning; you may always stop for breath and then repeat from a good place.

- [[ۘ]] (meem): stop here. {{2:26:28}}
- [[ۙ]] (lā): do not stop here. {{2:25:18}}
- [[ۗ]] (qalā): stopping is better. {{2:13:12}}
- [[ۚ]] (jīm): stopping and continuing are equal. {{2:19:16}}
- [[ۖ]] (ṣalā): continuing is better. {{2:5:5}}
- [[ۛ]] (three dots, twice): stop at one of the two, not both. {{2:2:4}} and {{2:2:5}}

## Screen 2: Signs in other prints

IndoPak prints use a few more letters: [[ط]], stopping is advised; [[ز]], stopping is allowed but continuing is better; [[ص]], stopping is allowed for breath but continuing is better; and [[ع]] in the margin marks the end of a section (rukūʿ). Both prints also mark each quarter, half and three quarters of a juz.

## Answer: The small meem

The meem sign, waqf lāzim, marks a stop needed for the meaning.
