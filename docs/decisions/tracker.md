# Tracker

## Current state
- `:tracker` is pure Kotlin. `JsonReferencePhonemes` reads `ordered_quran_phonemes.json` (schema 2) with `org.json` from the android-stubs jar; fields are listed in `tracker/README.md`.
- Alignment unit is one character of a model token, with runs of one repeated character merged (hearing and reference side, word by word). All 41 reference characters exist in `tokens.txt`.
- `AlignmentTracker` aligns the unsettled heard characters to the words from the first unsettled word to the end of the ayah two after it (semi-global Needleman-Wunsch, affine gaps: match 4, substitution -3, insertion open -5 extend -1, deletion open -5 extend 0).
- Judgement: green when `WordScore` passes: cost = (sum of substitution costs + missing x 1 + extra x 0.5) / word length, allowed 0.22 for 3 phonemes or fewer, +0.02 per phoneme, at most 0.34. Near sounds (emphatic/plain, interdental/alveolar, throat, back-of-mouth consonants, the three short vowels) cost 0.4 to 0.5, other swaps 1. Red at 2 or more substitutions once the user is heard in a later word; Tentative only while the word may still be said. A word that settles unconfirmed becomes Wrong, so a passed word is Confirmed or Wrong. A word is settled when a later word has 2 aligned phonemes and at least 50 % of the word matched; otherwise it waits.
- `WordState.Mispronounced` (confirmed word with a substitution event or a non-Ok madd) is display only: the tracker never emits it, the recite session publishes it. Madd Underheld needs the vowel under 0.4 x expected as well as a haraka short (Husary reads long madds at about half the rule time: 65 of 563 clean words were yellow before, 14 now).
- Entry check: nothing is judged until the aligned heard phonemes include `entryMatches` matches (7 patient to 5 eager, 6 at 0.5), at least 70 % of the heard ones, with at most 2 expected phonemes left out among them; before that only the latest 16 heard phonemes are kept. Seeded random phonemes (bursts of 2 to 12, streams of 300, surahs 1, 112, 2): 0 of 300 runs colour a word (before: 165 of 300 for 2 phonemes, 278 for 8).
- Skip: words with no aligned phoneme, flagged when a later word is green and matched phonemes after them reach `skipEvidencePhonemes` (8 - 5 x speed). Skipped words get `WordState.Wrong` plus `TrackerEvent.Skipped`.
- Insertion: a run of unmatched heard characters (extra or substituted) of `insertionPhonemes` (8 - 4 x speed, 6 at 0.5). Re-anchor: the last `reanchorPhonemes` (16 - 8 x speed, 12 at 0.5) have at most 75 % matches, then a local search of the loaded words with the last 24 characters; accepted at 60 % of a perfect match and 16 points above the current alignment.
- `currentIndex()` counts a red word as passed (the contract said "confirmed or skipped").
- Substitution events: one per substituted phoneme of a passed word, with the model symbols (the reference has no letter positions, so heard and expected are phoneme characters).
- Duration events: madd annotations come from pairing the `rules` ids 1 to 6 with the runs of two or more of ا ۥ ۦ in order; ayahs where counts or lengths disagree get none. One haraka = 0.35 x median gap of the last 40 short-vowel tokens; verdict outside expected +- 1 haraka.
- `Tracker.append(words)` adds reference words after the loaded ones without a reset: states, anchor, heard phonemes and symbol numbers stay, and new words take the next indexes (`buildReferenceTables` no longer clears the symbol table; `load` does). The recite session calls it when fewer than 15 words are left, so reciting crosses pages and surahs. Tests: five in `AlignmentTrackerTest`, including "appending in three steps gives the same events as loading everything at once".
- Contract additions to `Tracker.kt`: `MaddAnnotation`, `ReferenceWord.madds` (default empty), `DurationVerdict`, `DurationCheck`, `TrackerEvent.Duration`, `TrackerEvent.Substitution`.
- Measured on Husary (`TrackingFixtureTest`, model v3), before -> after WordScore: green 87 of 87 -> 87 of 87 on surahs 1, 112, 113, 114 (gate 83); 2:1-20 228 -> 229 of 230, 36:1-20 159 -> 159 of 159; wrong-ayah anchors 0 -> 0; false skips 0 -> 0 on 10 unmodified fixtures; cut ayah flagged 10 -> 10 of 10 (gate 9); foreign segment flagged 7 -> 9 of 10 (gate 7); slowed vowels 8 -> 8 of 10; original madds not Ok 0 of 16 both. Clean recordings (6 full, 4 at tempo 1.2, 563 words): yellow by sound 2, by madd only 14 (pausal lengthening, Overheld), red 1, tentative at the end 0.
- Madd duration: a vowel ends at the start of the next token that matched one of the next 2 expected phonemes (the model puts extra tokens inside a held vowel). 0 of 16 original madds flagged; 8 of 10 vowels slowed 3 times flagged Overheld (gate 9; was 5, of which one was the harness stretching the wrong madd). `DurationCheck.onsetSeconds` gives the vowel's onset. Substitutions: 0 on clean short surahs, 2 on the two long sets (1 each), emitted near the splice in 3 of 10 spliced fixtures.
- Fixtures: 62 ayah WAVs, 6 full, 10 cut, 10 splice, 4 + 2 stretched; foreign segments are 1.3 to 1.8 s (one to three words), the start of another surah's ayah.
- Model permission: the Quran-Lab authors approved the maintainer's request to use Zipformer P-Arabic v3 from Hugging Face (Quran-Lab/zipformer_p-arabic-v3, NPL-1.2), 2026-10-21. awn-models came from another copy: both .onnx files match Hugging Face in size (72,705,392 bytes); tokens.txt is the same text with CRLF endings; ordered_quran_phonemes.json is a separately generated file (quran-transcript, schema 2, 12.8 MB) and differs from the 5.1 MB file on Hugging Face.
- Voice search (`AyahFinder`, Search screen mic): local alignment of the heard phonemes against all 6,236 ayahs joined (652,401 phonemes, repeats collapsed), credited to the ayah where the match starts, +8 when it starts at an ayah's first phoneme. Each `AyahMatch` also carries `firstWord` and `lastWord` (0-based): the words of that ayah the best alignment covers, from a per-phoneme word table beside the ayah table and the start and end phoneme kept with each ayah's best score; a recitation that runs into the next ayah ends at the ayah's last word. Two columns of memory. Listening stops on Stop, at 15 s, or when the same ayah led three 1 s searches and 5 s were heard. Measured 2026-10-24 (AyahFinderAccuracyTest, JVM): at 5 s, right ayah first 90% (40 Alafasy/Minshawy ayahs from tools/fetch_search_fixtures.py) and 95% (62 Husary), in the top three 98% both; at 3 s 68%/55% first. Slowest search 81 ms at 5 s on the PC (unchanged by the word span); not yet timed on the phone. Misses: openings shared by many ayahs (4:135 "يا أيها الذين آمنوا") and ayahs under 8 phonemes (36:1).

## Gotchas
- `OnlineRecognizerResult.ysProbs` is empty for CTC greedy search; `SherpaPhonemeSource` reports probability -1.
- Feed about 1 s of silence after the last block (or call `finish()`): the last 0.48 s chunk is not decoded otherwise.
- Token onsets lie on a 0.04 s grid and about 0.12 s after the sound in the file.
- Foreign clips of 0.6 to 0.9 s produced zero or one tokens (splices flagged 4 of 10); clips need 1 s or more.
- Gradle daemons are shared: use `-Dorg.gradle.daemon.registry.base=/tmp/tracker-daemons` so another agent's `--stop` cannot kill a test run. `spotlessApply` and `detekt` exist only at the root project.

## Tried / rejected
- Madd from the gap before the vowel token, or the sum before + after: originals reach 7.4 haraka (1:7 word 5) like slowed vowels (5 to 9.7); no separating line.
- Madd from the second next token minus one short-vowel gap: false Overheld on original 1:7 word 5 (0.28 s against 0.21 s allowed); 113:4 stays out of reach because the stretched ayah inflates its own median gap.
- Overheld tolerance of 0.6 haraka instead of 1 (2026-10-21): 3 of 16 original madds flagged (gate 0) and slowed vowels flagged 7 of 8 candidates, because the set of Ok originals shrank; no gain, so the tolerance stays 1 haraka. Madd stays at 8 of 10.
- Voice-activity gate for noise: the model alone answers 26 synthetic noises with 0 phonemes (one for loud clicks); the cause was stray phonemes reaching the alignment.
- Deletion extend cost -1: a skipped 100-phoneme ayah cost 105 points, so the re-anchor search fired first and picked a repeated phrase in a later ayah (one wrong anchor, cut fixtures 8 of 10).
- Strictly unmatched tail for re-anchoring: chance matches in a 26-letter alphabet keep the run short; use the match share instead.
- Settling poorly matched words: let noise march the anchor forward through red words; they now wait.
- Uniform time stretching to test the madd check: scales the reference short vowels too, so the verdict cannot change.
