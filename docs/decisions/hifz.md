# Hifz

## Current state
- `:feature:hifz` owns `HifzDatabase` (Room, file `hifz.db`, version 2, schemas in `feature/hifz/schemas`, `HifzMigrationTest`): `plan` (one row: method, lesson size, Manzil cycle, recent days, ayahs a day, revision page limit, cursor ayah, print layout, start day), `page_progress` (status 0..3, learned_on, last_revised_on, FSRS columns), `session` (saved parts of a day: pages given, weakest grade, `is_complete`, `duration_millis`), `session_ayah` (each lesson ayah's firmness), `session_page` (each revision page's grade), `mistake` (ayah, kind, time). Days are epoch days.
- FSRS lives in `:core:data` (`core.data.fsrs`), shared with `:feature:learn`. Pages use `PageProgressRules.dailyScheduler()` (no minute steps). A page is Solid from 21 days of stability; Forgot sends a page back to Learning.
- Methods are pure Kotlin in `feature.hifz.method`. `AssignmentGenerator.generate` builds a day from the plan, the page records and the parts finished today, and keeps nothing from earlier days. Sabaq, Sabqi, Manzil: Sabqi is pages taught in the last `recentDays` (not today), Manzil is the old pages ordered oldest revised first, `ceil(old pages / cycle)` a day; both share `revisionPageLimit`, Sabqi first. 3 by 3, Ayah chain and Fixed ayahs give a lesson of N ayahs and revise pages whose memory is due. No method gives nothing.
- Lessons (`LessonPlanner`) follow the cursor: a page lesson is the ayahs that start on the page, a half page is half of them (first half rounded up), ayah lessons may cross pages; known pages are skipped. A Forgot grade keeps the cursor so the lesson returns. A page is Memorised when its last ayah is inside a graded lesson, else Learning.
- Pages follow the user's print: `PageLayoutFamily` (Madani 1421 for QPC Hafs, V2 and tajweed; Madani 1405 for V1; IndoPak 15 lines has a family of its own). `PageTable` is built from `QuranTextRepository.ayahsOnPage`; the plan stores its family and `HifzProgress.remap` moves records when the print changes family (new page takes the record of the old page that holds its first ayah).
- `HifzTab(controller, quranText, onStartPractice, onOpenAyah, showTopBar, topBarActions)` is the tab (own top bar by default). Pages: main (Method link row, Today rows with minutes or Done, revision load bar `N of LIMIT pages`, one primary button for the next undone part, progress map of one row per juz and four colours taken from the mode's field palette, Mark what I know, Weak spots), method chooser (also opened from the Method row, with the method's settings and Revision limit), Mark what I know (by surah, juz or pages, then a state). `rememberHifzTodayLine(controller)` gives the one line for the Quran tab ("Sabaq page 24, revise 4 pages", or Done; null without a plan or with No method). `HifzController` holds `screenState`, `practice`, `startPractice(kind)`, `startNextPart()`, `finishPractice(grade)`.
- Hifz bar: one row of a part pill (name and ayah, menu with End part and Leave without saving), hide words, previous, play, next, repeat, mic, all 44 dp; while words are hidden a second row (right aligned) adds show next word and show ayah, drawn by `ReciteRevealButtons` from :feature:recite (same session calls, icons and hint-letter step as the recite bar; the row had no room for two more buttons on 360 dp). Listening and knowledge: the Hifz bar's Play plays a `ListenWindow` (starts as the part; Previous and Next ayahs move it by its length across surahs, clipped at the Quran's ends). `HifzRepository.setKnownUpTo(table, ayah)` ("Memorised up to" rows on the Method page and on Mark what I know) moves the cursor after the ayah and marks unknown pages wholly before it Memorised.
- Weak spots: an ayah with the same mistake (skipped or another word) twice or more in the last 7 days, five at most.
- Practice: `HifzController.startPractice(kind)` stores a `PracticeRequest`; :app calls `AwnServices.startHifzPractice(request)` (`recite.practiceFrom`: a new lesson shows the words and the bar's eye hides them, revision parts start hidden; hiding lasts for the run and never changes the saved recite setting; Quran screen opened at the first ayah). `QuranScreenSlots.hifzBar` (set by :app only while `controller.practice` is non-null) replaces the recite bar in recite mode; `ReciteBarOrDownload` wraps it so the speech download card still comes first. `HifzBar` has a title row (part, ayah or pages, Finish, Close) and a control row (show or hide words, play the part with repeat x1/3/5/10/20, microphone). Finish shows Easy, Good, Hard, Forgot with the suggestion (`HifzProgress.suggestedGrade` from flagged words) filled; one tap records the part (`finishPractice`). Leaving the bar restores the player's repeat setting, exits recite and drops an unfinished practice.
- A stopped recitation writes its flagged words as mistakes (`PracticeMistakes`): skipped word and wrong word only; unclear words, letter substitutions and timing are not counted.

- End part opens `EndPartSheet`: one row per lesson ayah (Not yet, Shaky, Firm, Very firm) or per revision page (Not yet or a grade), pre-set from the recitation (`EndPartSuggestions`; no recitation means Firm or Good), Save and Keep practising pinned. Only the run of memorised ayahs from the lesson's start moves the cursor; a page is Memorised when all its ayahs are in the run, graded by its weakest ayah (Shaky = Hard, Firm = Good, Very firm = Easy). A partly saved lesson or revision stays on Today with what is left.
- Revision spacing (`RevisionSpacing`, plan columns `revision_spacing`, `spacing_steps`): Manzil cycle (Sabaq default), Fixed steps (user-edited days, page `spacing_step`; Good +1, Easy +2, Hard same, Forgot 0) or Adaptive (FSRS due, default of the other methods); `DueRevision` picks old pages, most overdue first, at the daily limit. Sabqi stays daily.
- Journal (60 days, filters, week strip of minutes), This week numbers (no streak) and the map's page sheet (tap a square; screen readers get one action per juz row). Marking keeps a known page's history and spreads first revisions of pages marked together over up to 30 days (`PageMarking`).

## Open items
- Mark what I know: multi-select of several surahs (mock 7) is not built; history keeping and the 30-day spread are.
- IndoPak 15-line pages are family `IndoPak15Lines` (the 16-line family was removed with its print).
- Minutes per part are rough constants (`PartMinutes`).

## Gotchas
- A forgotten page needs a card to be promoted again by a Good revision; half lessons have no card on purpose.

## Tried / rejected
- Carrying overdue pages to the next day: it piles up and users quit when revision piles up.
