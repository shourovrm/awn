# Learn

## Current state
- `:feature:learn` owns `LearnDatabase` (Room, file `learn.db`, version 2). Version 1 holds `event(kind, surah, ayah, page, at)` and `card(surah, ayah, stability, difficulty, due, last_review, reps, lapses, state, step)`; `MIGRATION_1_2` adds the course tables `word_card`, `grammar_card`, `queued_word`, `lesson_done`, `learn_set`, `learn_step` and `session_log` (DAO `WordDao`). `step` is one column beyond the plan, because FSRS needs the learning step between reviews. Schemas are exported to `feature/learn/schemas`; `LearnMigrationTest` opens every old schema.
- `LearnProgress` implements `ProgressEvents`. :app gets it with `learnProgressEvents(context)` and passes it to :feature:read and :feature:recite. `hifzCompleted` rates the card by itself: no skips Good, one skip Hard, two or more Again. `gradeAyah(ayah, rating)` is the user's own grade from the recite bar or the Revision screen: within 10 minutes of an automatic rating of the same ayah it restores the card kept in memory before rating, so one recitation counts as one review; otherwise it equals `rateCard`. `dueAyahs()` lists due ayahs in reading order.
- Derived figures (`ProgressCalculator`, pure functions): streak is consecutive local days with any event, ending today or yesterday; pages this week are distinct pages per local day summed over today and the six days before; an ayah is memorised after `memorisationThreshold` (default 3, range 1 to 10, in `LearnSettings` DataStore) skip-free completions on that many different days; cards are due when their local due date is today or earlier.
- The day boundary is the device's midnight, read from `ZoneId.systemDefault()` on every call.
- `Fsrs.kt` (in :core:data, package `core.data.fsrs`, shared with :feature:hifz) ports py-fsrs 6.3.2 (default 21 weights, fuzzing removed). `tools/generate_fsrs_vectors.py` writes `core/data/src/test/resources/fsrs_vectors.json` (200 default sequences, 50 without learning steps, py-fsrs version and git commit recorded); `FsrsReferenceTest` requires equal due times to the second. The app runs FSRS without minute-long learning steps (`dailyScheduler`), so a new or forgotten ayah is due after at least one day.
- Tajweed is `content/lessons/{en,bn}/tajweed/` (units 11 to 19, `course: tajweed`), drafts with `reviewed_by` empty; the twelve older JSON lessons and their loader are gone. `LegacyLessonProgress` adds a unit to the done set when every older lesson it covers is done (older Reading ids map too: letters-03-short-vowels to letters-04, tanween to 05, long-vowels to 07, sukoon to 08, shaddah to 09, first-ayah to 10, letters-by-name to 03) (11: makharij, sifat; 12: raa, lam-of-allah; 13: noon-sakinah-tanween; 14: meem-sakinah; 15: ghunnah; 16: qalqalah; 17: madd-natural, madd-extended; 18: hamzat-al-wasl; 19: waqf-signs); `observeSnapshot` applies it on every read.
- `src/main/assets/snippets.json` holds 34 ayahs and 26 hadith. `tools/build_snippets.py` writes it; `tools/check_snippets.py` checks it against sunnah.com and the hadith API. English and Bangla renderings are the project's own drafts.
- Lesson and screen text never says the app checks ghunnah, qalqalah, tafkheem or other sifat; live feedback covers skipped words, substituted letters and madd length only. `LessonContentTest` scans the lesson bodies.
- The course screens get `AwnTheme(field = Paper)` from `LearnHost` (`CoursePage`); without it they drew under the status bar.
- `LearnScreen(quranText, translations, packs, downloads, modifier, onReviseAt, barActions)` is the Learn tab (`barActions` = Settings icon from :app). It routes with `LearnRoute` (package `ui`): the tab opens on `FirstOpen` until `LearnSettings.courseStarted`, then on `Hub`; `learnBackFrom` sends every screen back to the hub (a word page to the word list) and the hub or first-open screen out of the tab. `LearnRoute.CourseChoices` reopens the first-open screen (`FirstOpenScreen(initial = ...)`) from the hub row "Letters and daily time"; saving writes `includeLetters` and `sessionMinutes` only, so cards, progress and `courseStarted` stay and the next plan adds or drops the letter lessons. Counted nouns in strings are `<plurals>` (English one/other; Bangla one and other identical); no `PluralsCandidate` ignore remains. `CourseRoutes` (`ui/CourseHost.kt`) draws the course screens; the older reading progress page, tajweed lesson list, one tajweed lesson and in-tab Revision stay in `LearnHost.kt`. `LocalLearnEnvironment` gives the screens the learning Arabic, the translation and the surah names. With `onReviseAt` set, "Revise ayahs" on the hub calls it with the first due ayah in reading order; without it the in-tab Revision runs (four grade buttons, Forgot = FSRS Again, Hard, Good, Easy).
- Word course: words are forms ranked by frequency (`word_forms.bin`), taught in sets of 10; `lessons.json` holds the basics, letters and grammar lessons. Both are generated at build time by `generateLearnContent` from `content/lessons` and the bundled Quran text. `LearnServices` loads them once, off the main thread (`courseAssets()`), and holds `WordCourseRepository`; `planToday` runs `CoursePlanner` at the measured pace. A plan puts reviews first but at most 65% of the time (`SessionPlanner.REVIEW_SHARE`); how well the user answers never blocks new words. `SessionController` runs a plan or a quiz one frame at a time; a quiz the user starts (`OwnQuiz`, from the quiz or word page) is only logged and never moves a card.
- Hub (`CourseHub`): today card, the tracks, progress, then the other rows; `buildHubState` makes the state. The course progress page (`CourseProgressScreen`) shows words known, the share of the Quran they cover and a coverage curve; progress is frequency coverage and "words met"/"words known", never surah completion. Word meanings need the word-by-word pack of the app language (`wbw_en` or `wbw_bn`); the hub offers the download through `PackDownloads` when it is missing.
- Today row: `learnCourseTodayLine(context)` gives `CourseTodayLine(minutes, newWords, reviews)` once the course has started; the single Learn row in :app then reads "Start today · N min" with new words and reviews, and shows the due-ayah revise line before that. Quran page word card: :app implements `core.data.MyWords` with `learnWordIsAdded` and `learnAddWord` (`WordCourseRepository.queueWord`), passed through `ReadDependencies.myWords`.
- Reminder: `ReviseReminder` sets one inexact `AlarmManager` alarm per day at the chosen time (settings `reminderEnabled` default off, hour 20, minute 0, in `LearnSettings`); `ReviseReminderReceiver` shows "N ayahs to revise" only when something is due and the notification permission is held, then sets tomorrow's alarm, and re-sets it after boot or an app update. `ReviseReminderSettingsPage()` is the Settings hub page (switch, time row, time dialog, asks for POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+). `ReviseReminder.rescheduleIfEnabled(context)` is for :app at start.
- Screens: `LearnBar` shows the back icon button with the screen title; figure tiles grow with text size (minimum 192 dp high); `RevisionAndProgressScreenTest` draws Revision and Progress 360 dp wide at font scale 2.0 in Light, Dark and Black.

- Word tiles: a lesson `words: <mark>` block (`LessonBlock.Words`, `ui/WordTiles.kt`) draws one or two neighbouring Quran words per tile, two tiles to a row (`AwnSpacing.wordTileHeight`), letters named by 1-based position (`ArabicLetters`, vowel marks belong to their letter) coloured with the mark's ink (`AwnLessonMarks`, day and night, from `MarkInks.kt`); a tap plays the words through `UnitSounds.playWord`, Play all plays every tile, the surah name opens the ayah through `LocalOpenAyah` (:app `openQuran`). `tip:` is a soft-fill aside. Tajweed units 11 to 18 use tiles (mockup `sessions/learn-redesign-mocks/a-tajweed-unit.html`); unit 19 keeps inline words because tiles drop the stop sign.
- Grammar track: basics 1 to 4, then grammar units ordered 4 to 16 (`03-pronoun-endings` is order 11); each unit has one `introduced_by` trigger chosen so the units fall after sets 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11, 13, 15, 16 and 35 by real form rank (`tools/build_learn_words.py`). Text follows Wright (1859); drafts, `reviewed_by` empty.
- Tracks (`course/LearnTracks.kt`): the hub lists Reading (`letters` lessons), Tajweed (`tajweed` lessons by `order`), Grammar (`basics` then `grammar`) and Quran words (`WordsTrackScreen`, below). A track page numbers its units; the first not done is next. Every unit of every track opens at any time; a user who chose the letters reads "Best after Reading unit 9" (shaddah) under the Tajweed title (rows that looked open but ignored taps read as a broken page). Plan and mockups: `sessions/round7-mocks/l5-*.html`.
- A unit opened from a track or the path (`LearnRoute.CourseUnit`, `ui/UnitScreen.kt`) is a `HorizontalPager`: teaching pages (a reading screen with the `## Grid` screens after it, drawn by `QaidaGrid` as five cells per row right to left; a tap or Play all plays the cell's clips through `UnitSounds` and lights the cell), every Check (the explanation shows after the first pick, which is the one that counts), the practice page when the unit has `## Practice` items, then a closing page with "Next unit". Practice (`PracticePage`) draws 10 items with `PracticeDraw` (seeded, seed saved with the screen) into a `PracticeRun`: one per screen, options as rows or, when every option is a lone `[[..]]`, 2-column tiles; after the answer the explanation shows and the clip (or the highlighted word through `WordSound`, which :app implements with the word reciter) plays; an item with `read_aloud` shows the Arabic, Listen and 0.75x buttons and counts as done. A clip value may join ids with `+`. Reaching the closing page marks the lesson done through `finishLesson`. `TajweedTrackTest` opens all 9 tajweed units from the hub.
- Reading units 1 to 10 (`content/lessons/{en,bn}/letters`) follow the Noorani Qaida's order: all letters, ba ta tha, letters by name, short vowels, tanween, small long vowels, madd and leen, sukoon, shaddah, a whole ayah. Their clip ids are checked with `build_learn_content.py --clips <folder>`.

- Quran words page (`ui/WordsTrackScreen.kt`, mocks `sessions/learn-redesign-mocks/b-` and `c-`): known and met words with the share of the Quran, today's set card (ten words in the learning font, meanings or the download offer, Start), "From your pages" (last read page and today's Hifz page, from `LearnPageLinks` that :app gives `LearnScreen`; Hifz row hidden without a plan), then only sets and one grammar line after the set that brings it (`PathInput.includeStartLessons = false`); Word list and Quiz stay at the bottom. A set opens `LearnRoute.SetPage` (ten words); a word opens its page and Back returns to the set or the page (`WordPage.from`).
- Words on a page (`LearnRoute.PageWords(page)`, `ui/PageWordsScreen.kt`): each distinct form once with meaning, count in the Quran and New/Learning/Known; filters carry counts; "Learn N new" queues the new forms (`queueNewWords`); "Open page" calls `LearnPageLinks.onOpenPage`. `PageWordsSource` reads the page in the user's print and falls back to the default print when its layouts are missing. Reached from the words page, the Quran page menu ("Words on this page") and the Hifz part menu, through `TabNavigation.showPageWords` and `LearnScreen(pageWordsRequest)`.
- "Show my words" (Quran page menu, `ReadSettings.showMyWords`, off by default): `core.data.MyWords.marks(words)` gives `MyWordMark.Known` or `Learning` per word key from the word cards (`PageWords.marks`); :feature:read fills those words behind the text in the Mushaf view (`PageDecorations.myWordMarks`) and in the line-by-line view, below recite and playing fills.

- Learn sounds: `learn-qaida-audio` (kind `learn-audio` in `scripts.json`, group `LearnAudio`) is a zip of `<unit>/<clip>.opus` extracted to `packs/learn-audio/<id>`; `PackRepository.learnAudioFile(clipId)` finds a clip; units with clips offer "Download sounds" on their first page until installed; Settings > Downloads lists it. Built by `tools/build_learn_audio_pack.py` and `build_manifests.py --add-learn-audio=<scripts.json>`.
- Lesson language: `LearnSettings.lessonLanguage` (`LessonLanguageChoice`: follow app, English, Bangla; default follow app) is resolved once in `LearnScreen` with `LearnLanguage.resolve` and decides lesson text, unit titles, grid transliterations, practice, word meanings (`wbw_en`/`wbw_bn`), the ayah translation and surah names inside Learn. Buttons and labels stay in the app language. Done ids are lesson ids, so switching never touches progress. The row is `LessonLanguageSettingsRow` on the Recite settings page; the Quran tab snippet stays in the app language.

## Gotchas
- Compose tests under Robolectric run on Android 15 (`@Config(sdk = [35])`): Espresso calls `InputManager.getInstance()`, which Android 16 removed.
- `check_snippets.py` finds the hadith number in the sunnah.com page, which proves little for short numbers; the hadith API comparison is the stronger check.
- Muslim numbers with sub-hadith use sunnah.com's letter form (`804a`); the hadith API calls the same report `804.01`.
